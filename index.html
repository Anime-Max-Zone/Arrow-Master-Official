FAQ

Common Questions

Is Arrow Master free?

Yes, the game is completely free. There are no hidden charges or subscriptions.

Is it available on the Play Store?

Currently, it is distributed via direct APK (GitHub Release). The Play Store version is coming soon.

APK is not installing, what should I do?

Give your browser "Install unknown apps" permission (Settings → Apps → Special access). Then open the file again.

How to play the game?

Clear the arrows on the screen by tapping them in the right direction. As levels increase, puzzles get harder.

Is my data safe?

Absolutely. The app does not collect any personal data. Only basic info is used for the leaderboard.
